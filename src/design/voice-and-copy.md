# Voice & copy guide (Phase-0 deliverable)

## Who is talking
An engineer who has actually been in the room where staff-level calls get made,
and who now teaches. Direct, opinionated, unimpressed by hype, generous with the
*why*. Writes the way a respected staff engineer talks in a design review: plain,
concrete, occasionally blunt. Never a marketer, never a cheerleader.

## The thesis we lead with (and never soften into mush)
> Getting better at writing code stops being enough somewhere around Senior.
> The promotion past it is decided in the part AI can't judge for you — the 30%.
> This is where we train.

Note the honest framing: code depth doesn't *stop mattering* (Solvers and
Architects go deep). It stops being *sufficient*. Never overclaim.

## Rules
1. **Concrete over abstract.** "Write the Alternatives-Considered section" beats "improve your communication."
2. **Show the delta, not a grade.** Tell someone the one behavior between them and the next level.
3. **Earn every claim.** If we cite the Dropbox ladder, it's because it says the thing — not for decoration. One citation, not five.
4. **No hedging apologies.** We don't say "this model is contested, sorry." We say what we measure and how.
5. **Bilingual by authorship.** ES is written by a person who works in ES, not translated. Spanish reads like a LATAM senior engineer wrote it, not like a localized SaaS.
6. **Punctuation discipline.** Em-dashes are seasoning, not the meal. Kill the "not X, but Y" tic. No exclamation marks except a single earned one at a threshold moment.
7. **Names from the profession.** The Room. Field Work. Signal. The Star Chart. Cadence. Cohort. Cross the threshold. Never boss/XP/quest.

## Microcopy tone examples
- Empty state (no assessment yet): "You haven't been placed yet. Twenty minutes, no login, honest result."  /  ES: "Todavía no tienes diagnóstico. Veinte minutos, sin cuenta, resultado honesto."
- After a confident-wrong: "You were sure, and it was wrong. That's the most useful thing that happened today — here's the misconception."  / ES: "Estabas seguro y fue un error. Es lo más útil que pasó hoy: aquí está la idea equivocada."
- Threshold crossing: "You crossed into L5 territory on System Design. One axis, real evidence."  / ES: "Cruzaste a territorio L5 en Diseño de Sistemas. Un eje, con evidencia real."
